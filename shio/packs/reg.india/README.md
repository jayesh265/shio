## Limits

Draft. Not reviewed by counsel; nothing in this pack is a compliance claim. Requirements are paraphrased from the
spec's summary of primary sources checked in November 2026 and must be re-read against the primary text before
review. Planned controls are not enforced: the coverage matrix (`shio preflight coverage`) shows them as not yet built.
Attestations prove that a named person signed that a duty was done, not that it was done well.

The pack runs in advisory mode (failures recorded, not enforced) until the DPDP Act's core duties are enforceable (May 2027 on the published timeline), unless the compliance context lists it in `enforce_advisory`.
