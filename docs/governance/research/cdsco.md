# Research notes: CDSCO and software as a medical device in India

**Status: research.** No controls until counsel has answered the questions below. The `in.cdsco` pack has no
controls. Shio never decides whether software is a medical device.

## Questions for counsel

1. How do the Medical Devices Rules, 2017 and CDSCO's notifications treat standalone software, and decision-support
   software in particular?
2. Which risk class would agents like the reference agents fall in, if they were devices, and what follows for
   quality management, clinical evaluation and post-market surveillance?
3. What evidence formats does CDSCO expect (technical files, clinical evidence, vigilance reports), and can Shio's
   safety case, records and safety-event records map to them?
4. How should `med.cds`'s posture constraints be worded for India so they read as enforcement of a declared design,
   not as a classification?
5. Are there sandbox or pilot routes for AI tools in clinical settings that change any of the above?
